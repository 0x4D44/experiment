================================================================================
TECHNICAL HIGHLIGHTS
================================================================================

Random Number Generation:
  - Mersenne Twister (MT19937) algorithm
  - Period: 2^19937 - 1
  - Seeded from /dev/urandom (Unix) or time+PID fallback
  - Passes statistical randomness tests

Mathematical Algorithms:
  - Prime checking: Trial division (O(sqrt(n)))
  - Fibonacci detection: Perfect square method
  - Perfect square: Direct sqrt computation
  - Divisor counting: Efficient factorization
  - Digit sum: Integer arithmetic

AI Algorithms:
  - Binary search: O(log n) convergence
  - Probabilistic: Weighted random selection
  - Machine learning: Pattern-based adaptation
  - All strategies mathematically sound

Scoring System:
  - Base points: 100 - (2 * guesses)
  - Time bonus: 5 pts/sec under 60s (max 30)
  - Difficulty multiplier: 1x to 16x
  - Hint penalty: -10 per hint
  - Challenge streak bonus: up to 3x multiplier

Difficulty Scaling:
  - Range = 10 * (2^(level-1))
  - Max guesses = 5 + (level * 2.5)
  - Mathematically fair progression
  - Optimal guesses = ceil(log2(range)) + 1
