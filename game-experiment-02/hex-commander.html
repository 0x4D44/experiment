Hex Commander

Turn-Based Hexagonal Strategy Game

How to Play

  • Select Unit: Click on one of your red units (Player 1)
  • Move: Click on a highlighted blue square to move the unit
  • Attack: Right-click on an enemy unit to attack
  • End Turn: Click "End Turn" button or the AI will take its turn
  • Objective: Eliminate all enemy units or hold more resource nodes (gold squares)
Unit Types: Infantry (I) - Balanced | Cavalry (C) - Fast | Archer (A) - Long Range
