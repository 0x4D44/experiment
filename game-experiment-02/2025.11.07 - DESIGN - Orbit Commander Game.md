# Orbit Commander - Design Document

## Overview
Orbit Commander is a space navigation strategy game that challenges players to successfully navigate spacecraft through a solar system using realistic orbital mechanics. Players must master gravity assists, fuel management, and trajectory prediction to complete increasingly difficult missions.

## Core Mechanics

### 1. Physics Engine
- **Gravitational Attraction**: All celestial bodies exert gravitational force according to Newton's law of universal gravitation
  - Force = G * (m1 * m2) / r²
  - Simplified constant G = 5.0 for game balance
- **Velocity & Acceleration**: Spacecraft accumulate velocity and acceleration based on gravitational forces
- **Trajectory Prediction**: Real-time line rendering showing predicted path 100 physics steps ahead
- **Time Acceleration**: Players can speed up time (1x, 2x, 5x, 10x) to see results faster

### 2. Spacecraft Controls
- **Launch Angle**: 0-360 degrees
- **Launch Velocity**: 0-100 units/second
- **Fuel System**:
  - Limited fuel for course corrections
  - Visible fuel bar
  - Fuel consumption only during active thrust
- **Thrust Controls**: Arrow keys or WASD for real-time course corrections

### 3. Celestial Bodies
- **Sun** (Center): Mass 1000, yellow, immobile
- **Mercury**: Mass 50, small gray
- **Venus**: Mass 100, orange
- **Earth**: Mass 150, blue, with Moon
- **Mars**: Mass 75, red
- **Moon** (orbits Earth): Mass 10, gray
- **Asteroid Belt**: Multiple small asteroids

### 4. Mission System
- **10 Missions of Increasing Difficulty**:
  1. Reach Mars from Earth (basic)
  2. Reach Venus with fuel efficiency bonus
  3. Deploy satellite to moon orbit
  4. Gravity assist to outer system
  5. Multi-body trajectory to Mercury
  6. Complex fuel management challenge
  7. Time-critical mission to asteroid
  8. Dual-planet rendezvous
  9. Grand tour (visit 4 planets)
  10. Impossible challenge mode

### 5. Scoring System
- **Base Score**: 1000 points per mission completed
- **Fuel Efficiency Bonus**: (Remaining Fuel / Max Fuel) * 500
- **Time Bonus**: (Time Limit - Elapsed Time) * 10 (if time limit exists)
- **Difficulty Multiplier**: Increases per mission (1x to 3x)

## UI/UX Components
1. **Game Canvas**: Central 800x600px viewport
2. **Control Panel**: Launch angle, velocity, fuel display
3. **Trajectory Preview**: White line showing predicted path
4. **Mission Status**: Current objective and progress
5. **Score Display**: Real-time score and statistics
6. **Time Controls**: Speed up/slow down simulation
7. **Status Messages**: Warnings and mission feedback

## Technical Architecture

### Physics System
- Updates at 60 FPS with dt=1/60
- Uses Verlet integration for stability
- Calculates forces for each body pair
- Handles collision detection with tolerance

### Game State
- Spacecraft position, velocity, fuel
- All celestial body positions
- Mission status and objectives
- Player score and statistics

### Rendering
- Canvas 2D context
- Particle effects for thrust
- Scale factors for visibility (visual scale != physics scale)
- Zoom controls optional

## Success Criteria
1. All 10 missions must be playable
2. Realistic orbital mechanics (no magic)
3. Trajectory prediction accurate within 100 steps
4. Fuel management matters
5. Score increases with difficulty
6. No crashes or infinite loops
7. Responsive controls
8. Clear mission objectives
