## Controls Reference

### Launch Phase (Before Launch)
- **Left/Right Arrow or A/D**: Rotate launch angle
- **Up/Down Arrow or W/S**: Adjust launch velocity
- **SPACEBAR**: Launch spacecraft

### Flight Phase (After Launch)
- **Up Arrow or W**: Activate thruster
- **A/D or Left/Right Arrow**: Rotate thrust direction
- **1/2/5/0**: Time scale (1x, 2x, 5x, 10x)
