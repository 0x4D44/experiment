## Game Summary

You are a detective trapped in a time loop. Each day repeats from 6:00 AM to 6:00 AM, but evidence you collect persists. Your goal: gather clues that point to the truth and break the loop.

- **5 NPCs** with daily routines and secrets
- **7 Evidence pieces** with specific collection requirements
- **3 Different Endings** based on evidence combinations
- **Multiple Solution Paths** for non-linear gameplay
- **Time Loop Mechanics** with persistent inventory
