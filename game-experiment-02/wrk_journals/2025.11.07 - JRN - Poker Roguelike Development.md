### Implementation Details

- Consistent damage values (100 for Royal Flush down to 2 for High Card)

**Game Progression**
- 10 encounters with increasing difficulty
- Enemy health scales from 50 HP to 150 HP (boss)
- Gold rewards: 10 + (5 × encounter number)
- Shop system with 4 upgrade options

**Combat System**
- Players select 5 cards from their hand
- Enemy draws random cards
- Both evaluate poker hands
- Damage dealt based on hand strength
- Combat continues until enemy defeated or player health reaches 0

### How to Play

1. Open `poker-quest.html` in a web browser
2. Click "START NEW RUN"
3. For each encounter:
   - Click on 5 cards to select them
   - Click "ATTACK" to play your hand
   - Cards with strong poker hands deal more damage
4. After each victory (except the final), visit the shop to buy upgrades
5. Defeat all 10 encounters including the Dragon Lord boss to win

### Game Balance

The game successfully balances:

- **Difficulty**: Progressive scaling prevents early dominance

### Design Decisions

1. **Poker Hand Damage Model**: Royal Flush (100 damage) provides powerful incentive to build strong hands while High Card (2 damage) keeps basic outcomes viable
2. **Scaling Difficulty**: Linear health scaling (50+10n) provides steady challenge
3. **Gold Economy**: Affordable early upgrades with expensive late-game options creates meaningful choices
4. **Shop System**: Four distinct upgrade types (Health, Healing, Refresh, Multiplier) provide strategic depth
