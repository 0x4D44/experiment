- Damping (0.98/frame) and friction for realism

## Challenges Encountered & Solutions

### Challenge 1: Physics Parameter Tuning
**Problem**: Initial settings caused unrealistic ball movement
**Solution**: Iteratively adjusted gravityConstant (tried 100-1000, settled on 500)
**Result**: Natural-feeling gameplay with appropriate challenge level

### Challenge 2: Collision Tunneling
**Problem**: High-speed ball could pass through obstacles
**Solution**: Implemented penetration detection and separation vector
**Result**: Reliable collision response at all speeds

### Challenge 3: Test Accuracy for Floats
**Problem**: Physics tests were too strict with exact float comparisons
**Solution**: Used epsilon comparisons and expected damping effects
**Result**: All 81 tests passing with realistic physics

### Challenge 4: Gravity Well Balance
**Problem**: Attractors too strong or too weak depending on distance
**Solution**: Properly implemented inverse-square law, tuned gravity constant
**Result**: Predictable gravity behavior for gameplay strategy
