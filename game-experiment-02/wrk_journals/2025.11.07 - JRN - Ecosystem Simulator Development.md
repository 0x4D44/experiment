## Game Mechanics

### Population Balance Requirements
- Grass: 100-1000 (if outside: imbalance)
- Rabbits: 10-200 (if outside: imbalance)
- Wolves: 2-30 (if outside: imbalance)
- Birds: 5-100 (if outside: imbalance)
- **Win Condition:** Maintain all in balance for 50 consecutive cycles
- **Loss Condition:** Any species reaches 0

### Weather System
- Normal: +1% water per cycle
- Drought: -5% water per cycle
- Abundant: +5% water per cycle
- Storm: -10% water per cycle
- Random weather changes (5% chance per cycle)
- 30% death rate if water < 30%
- 10% death rate if water < 50%

### Predation System
- Wolves hunt rabbits with 70% success rate
- Birds hunt rabbits with 50% success rate
- Birds forage grass (50% chance per cycle)
- Energy cost scales with metabolism
- Reproduction requires energy threshold
- Offspring inherit half parent's reproduction energy
